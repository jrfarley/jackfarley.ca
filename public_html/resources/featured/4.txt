--Title--
Epiphytes of Urban Vancouver

--Link--
https://open.library.ubc.ca/soa/cIRcle/collections/undergraduateresearch/51869/items/1.0398550

--Preview--
In my first year at UBC I was assigned a research project. My 
collaborator Ally Howard and I chose to do ours comparing the 
epiphytic community compositions of native and nonnative hardwood 
species on the UBC Vancouver Campus. Our paper was selected for 
publication in UBC’s online repository cIRcle.
