--Title--
Moths of Yanayacu

--Link--
yanayacu_moths

--Date--
April 1, 2012

--Preview--
In December 2021 I spent two and a half weeks volunteering at the Yanayacu 
Biological Station on the east slope of the Ecuadorian Andes. During my 
time there I photographed and identified close to 200 species of moths at 
the station’s light sheet.
