--Preview--
In 2023 I began working with Dr. Irwin from the UBC Zoology Department on a 
computer model of secondary contact between two populations with incomplete 
reproductive isolation. Our research focuses on the role of mate search costs 
and pleiotropy in preserving distinct mating types.
