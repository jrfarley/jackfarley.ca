--Title--
Dragonflies of the Sustainable Roots Reserve

--Link--
Odonate_Survey.pdf

--Preview--
I spent January to May or 2022 as an intern at the Sustainable Roots Foundation in Cosanga, Ecuador. During 
that time I conducted regular dragonfly surveys of the Sustainable Roots Reserve. I observed 10 different 
species at the reserve, several of which are endemic to the region.
