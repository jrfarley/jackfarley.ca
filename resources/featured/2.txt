--Title--
Birds of Tesoro Escondido

--Link--
tesoro_birds.html

--Preview--
In November 2021 I spent two weeks volunteering for the Jocotoco 
Foundation at Reserva Tesoro Escondido leading bird inventory 
efforts. During that time I recorded 228 species of birds including 
endangered species such as Great Green Macaw, Purple Quail-Dove, 
and Baudo Guan.
